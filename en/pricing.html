One page or several?

Google doesn't show whole websites. It shows pages. Each page has its own title and description in the search results, and that's what decides which searches it shows up in.

One page

It gets just one title on Google. It mostly shows up for people searching your business name and your main service.

Example: "Coast Tours Caraguatatuba".

Good if people already search for you by name and you do one main thing.

Several pages

Each service gets its own page with its own title. Each one can show up for a different search, even from people who've never heard of you.

Example: "boat tour Caraguatatuba", "boat rental Caraguatatuba", "Tamanduá Island tour".

Good if you offer several services or want to be found by people who don't know your name yet.

And languages: a tourist searching in English will only find a page written in English. Google can't read a button that swaps the language on the fly. That's why, in the Complete package, each language gets its own pages.
