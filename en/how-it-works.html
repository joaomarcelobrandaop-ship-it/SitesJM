First I research. Then I build.

Before I design anything, I study your business and your industry. You only decide after you've tried the finished site.

1

Research on your business

I look for your business the way a customer would: on Google, Maps and Instagram. I read your listing and your reviews, and I test every link.

  • Your Google rating and how many reviews you have
  • What shows up when people search your name
  • Broken links, or links that go to the wrong place
  • How your Instagram and WhatsApp are set up

What you get: a short analysis with real numbers, on WhatsApp.

2

Research on your industry

Every industry gets searched differently. I look into how people search for what you sell, what they ask, and what makes them pick one place over another. The site comes out of that research, not out of my personal taste.

An example: places to stay

For hostels and guesthouses, I did a big research project on how travelers search for and choose a place to stay. It turned into a checklist that every accommodation site I build has to meet. When I compared it with a site I'd already made, I found 8 things missing, including:

  • A photo of the place on the very first screen
  • Dates picked from a calendar, not typed in
  • Written reviews, not just the star rating
  • A section for people traveling in groups

What you get: a site built around what your customers are looking for.

3

Free preview

With both rounds of research done, I build a preview of your site, with your name, your services and your photos, working on mobile. It's free, and you're not committing to anything.

The WhatsApp buttons in the preview already go to your number, so you can test the exact message your customers will send. And the preview stays off Google until you decide.

Until you sign the contract, you can ask for as many changes as you like. The preview only becomes your site once it's exactly how you want it.

What you get: a link to the preview to open on your phone, and unlimited changes until you approve.

4

Contract and monthly care

Happy with it? We sign the contract and the site goes live on your own domain, in your name, ready to show up on Google and in AI answers.

After that, I make up to 2 small changes a month: swapping a photo, updating a price, tweaking some text. New pages or features are quoted separately.

What you get: your site live, plus 2 small changes a month.
